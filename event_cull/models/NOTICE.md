The bundled `emotion-ferplus-8.onnx` model is the FER+ emotion-recognition
model distributed by the ONNX Model Zoo.

- Source: https://huggingface.co/onnxmodelzoo/emotion-ferplus-8
- Model SHA-256: `a2a2ba6a335a3b29c21acb6272f962bd3d47f84952aaffa03b60986e04efa61c`
- License: Apache-2.0, as declared by the source repository metadata.

It runs locally on detected face crops and is used only to supplement smile
detection. Images are not uploaded by this feature.
